# Visual reference study

Inspected 2026-09-13. These are inspiration and source research, not reusable media licenses. The posts' model, prompt-count, and speed claims are their authors' claims; no controlled comparison was performed.

| Reference | What was visibly useful | Apply without copying |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Tejas / Atomik Growth](https://x.com/makwanatejas170/status/2096555742597988400) | White/lavender stage, phone panels in perspective, coordinated overlap around concise central copy. | Use depth to connect product surfaces. Keep the actual workflow readable and the important copy unobscured. |
| [Biemjo](https://x.com/Biemjo/status/2097023743478394932) | White space, a dark platform card, a restrained line leading to a blue output/cursor. | Establish input, action, and result with a small number of elements. Abstract icons need real product evidence nearby. |
| [Sumant Kael](https://x.com/SumantKael/status/2096612679700103236) | Short text/device shots: white grid, large type, a blue emphasis, angled device imagery. | Make one point per beat. Keep the hook readable without sound and give product screens enough viewing time. |

The signed-in browser exposed playable posts and representative frames. This study sampled visible frames; it is not a complete motion-quality audit of all three videos. Public text fetching returned 403. Do not claim to have inspected unavailable prompts or measured the authors' production times.

The linked [Atomik source](https://github.com/Tejashmakwana/astra-atomik-launch-video/tree/08d735aab8b20620ce903544bfa1392015f81975) was inspected for layout/motion techniques. Its custom license does not grant blanket commercial rights to media, music, fonts, or marks. No source or assets from that project are included here.

The Relay fixture is original fictional UI: scattered customer notes → one inbox → repeated request → scoped task → next action. It uses no real product claims, copied logos, stock footage, or music. Its direction can demonstrate the skill, but must not become a fixed style imposed on another product.
